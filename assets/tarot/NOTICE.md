# Tarot assets and card data

## Card data

Tarot card data by [Taro’s Tarot](https://tarostarot.com/tarot-card-data), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The bundled `source-v1.0.0.json` is the original dataset downloaded from <https://tarostarot.com/tarot-card-data/tarot-cards.json> on 2026-11-18. Its declared version is 1.0.0 and update date is 2026-11-12.

`deck.json` adapts that dataset for this plugin. The card names, eight keywords per card, and upright and reversed meanings have been translated into Simplified Chinese. The original English card names and stable identifiers are retained. Image locations are mapped to bundled JPG filenames. Astrology, elements, yes/no answers, and external image URLs have been omitted from the normalized file. These translations and adaptations are supplied under CC BY 4.0. No endorsement by Taro’s Tarot is implied.

When redistributing the plugin or its data, retain this notice and the attribution and licence links. If you further adapt the data, identify your changes.

## Card images

The 78 JPG card fronts in `cards/` come from [Rider-Waite Smith Tarot Cards (CC0) by LuciellaES](https://luciellaes.itch.io/rider-waite-smith-tarot-cards-cc0), using the user-provided `Cards-jpg` download. The pack contains 300 × 527 pixel scans of the original Rider-Waite-Smith deck, sourced by the pack author from Wikipedia, cleaned up, resized, and given a small black border. The original illustrations were drawn by Pamela Colman Smith for A. E. Waite and published in 1909.

The pack author identifies the original artwork as public domain under Australian law and separately releases the included cardback design under CC0. Only the 78 front images are bundled here. Credit for the pack’s preparation goes to Luciella Elisabeth Scarlett (LuciellaES). Her source page makes credit optional and asks that the images be incorporated into a work with additional labour rather than sold unchanged.

The runtime can rotate these fronts 180 degrees and compose them into a three-card image. The original bundled JPG files are unchanged.
